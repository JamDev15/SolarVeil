Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

SolarVeil Home Window Films ("SolarVeil," "we," "us") installs residential window film in the Denver metro area and along Colorado's Front Range. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you request an in-home consultation through our form, you may provide:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your street address, city and ZIP code
  • Details about your home, such as home style, number of windows, the film you are interested in and your preferred way to be contacted
  • Anything else you choose to write in the notes field

We also keep the information you share when you call, text or email us.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you view and the time of your visit. This is used to keep the site running and secure. Our website does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To contact you, schedule your in-home consultation and prepare a quote
  • To perform the installation work you request and follow up afterward
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support
  • To keep business records and meet legal obligations
  • To keep our website secure and working properly

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Service providers we use

We rely on a few outside services to run this website. Each receives only the information it needs to do its job:

  • FormSubmit delivers your consultation request from our website to our email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts our business email, where requests and messages are stored.
  • Vercel hosts this website and keeps standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash supply the fonts and some of the photos shown on the site. Your browser connects to them directly to load these files, which shares your IP address with them.
  • Google Search Console helps us see how our site appears in Google search results. It reports on search performance overall and does not identify individual visitors.

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect our rights or safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business.

How long we keep information

We keep consultation requests and customer records for as long as we need them to serve you, honor any warranty and meet tax and legal requirements. When we no longer need information, we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email or call us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time. Colorado residents may have additional rights under the Colorado Privacy Act where it applies.

Security

This website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we limit access to customer information to the people who need it. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Our services are for homeowners and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
